# Phase 1 — Painterly Graphics Foundation

## 1.1 Painterly art direction

The visual target will be a colorful illustrated strategy map:

- Gouache/watercolor-inspired color variation.
- Soft, textured brush strokes and subtle canvas grain.
- Rich but controlled saturation.
- Lighting consistently from the upper left.
- Soft lower-right shadows.
- Avoid heavy black outlines and flat single-color tiles.
- Terrain must remain identifiable by **shape and pattern**, not hue alone.

Proposed terrain treatment:

| Terrain | Painted treatment | Non-color identifier |
|---|---|---|
| Plains | Warm greens, ochre highlights, grass strokes | Small grass and flower marks |
| Forest | Layered emerald/olive canopies with deep shadows | Distinct painted tree clusters |
| Water | Teal/blue washes with reflected highlights | Curved wave and foam strokes |
| Mountain | Slate, umber, and warm stone highlights | Angular painted rock facets |

The first assets will be original painterly-style placeholders or suitably licensed painted assets. They will use final asset paths so better commissioned or CC0 artwork can replace them later without renderer changes.

## 1.2 Asset specifications and import rules

Logical tiles remain **64×64**, preserving gameplay coordinates and tests.

- Terrain source artwork: **128×128** per tile.
- Building illustrations: transparent source images, approximately **192×192**.
- Resource/UI icons: **64×64**, displayed at 24–32 pixels.
- Multiple plains, forest, water, and mountain variants.
- Linear texture filtering and mipmaps for zoomable world art.
- No nearest-neighbor/pixel-art import settings.
- Atlas padding to prevent painted-edge bleeding at lower mip levels.

Suggested structure:

```text
assets/
  sprites/
    terrain/
    buildings/
    units/
    ui/
    fx/
  fonts/
  sounds/
resources/
  tilesets/
  ui/
docs/
```

## 1.3 Replace procedural terrain rendering

Add a dedicated `TerrainRenderer` while keeping `map.gd` authoritative for gameplay data.

Planned rendering structure:

```text
Map
├── ResourceManager
├── TurnManager
├── TerrainRenderer
│   ├── GroundLayer
│   ├── TransitionLayer
│   └── DecorationLayer
├── BuildingLayer
└── SelectionOverlay
```

Implementation details:

- Use `TileMapLayer` and a painted `TileSet`.
- Add terrain variants selected deterministically from coordinates and map seed.
- Add neighbor-aware transitions:
  - Grass-to-water shorelines.
  - Forest edges.
  - Mountain/cliff boundaries.
- Add restrained decorative details such as flowers, stones, canopy highlights, and foam.
- Never use unseeded per-frame randomness.
- Preserve the existing procedural renderer as a `--low-gfx` fallback for tests and low-end machines.

A small HUD binding script will connect map signals to UI controls. Tests that currently assume `Map/TopBar` or `Map/TileInfoPanel` will be updated to use the new HUD paths. Save/load/end-turn signals will remain connected.
